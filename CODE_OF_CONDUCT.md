# Code of Conduct

Pi Desktop is committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of
experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome questions and differing technical perspectives.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Accept maintainers' decisions on project scope and moderation.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable in project spaces or in public
spaces when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when conduct is inappropriate. Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact,
and whether harmful behavior continues after a warning.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Inas1234). Do not include sensitive personal details
in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical, and no one
who makes a good-faith report will face retaliation from the project.
